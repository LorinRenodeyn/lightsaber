# The Opponent

During any tournament you should take the opportunity to study future opponents. What form do they prefer, are they righthanded or lefthanded, how much experience do they have? Lots of fencers will say that size is a factor, where it is not. So as Yoda said, ‘Size matters not’.

Sword fighting is one of the fairest fights there is. What we mean by this, is that the more training you put into the art, the more it will reward you. Being smaller in stature or taller, might seem to give you advantages in range, but if you fight skillfully, you can nullify this in your defense, where your blades will determine what distance counts. Both sizes might have different advantages, but combined they negate each other. Where the taller duelist can use his height for strikes, the smaller duelist as but to cover those openings and can in the meantime threaten the lower openings. Where the smaller duelist is nimbler in movement, the taller duelist will use lesser energy to counter. These are but two examples, but I feel they bring the point across.
