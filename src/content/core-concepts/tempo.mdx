# Tempo

Tempo, or the rhythm is a measurement of the speed at which you or your opponent perform an action. This means that your tempo can be greater, equal or smaller than the opponent’s tempo. One strike or step can be counting as one tempo, but if it gets blocked or interrupted, it could be halved. A trained fighter will have a steady tempo, but a more experienced fighter will play with his opponent by switching between a high or low tempo.

Here are a few examples of standard tempo measurements:

1. Blocking an opponent correctly
2. When the opponent strikes at you in vain and his blade passes you by
3. When the opponent lifts his arms up to strike
4. When the opponent, within striking distance, changes stance
5. When the opponent takes one full step
