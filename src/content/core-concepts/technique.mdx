# Technique 

For this concept you can ask yourself a few questions. What techniques do I master, which techniques does my opponent use and how can I confidently counter those? You could also call this your skill or arsenal. The first techniques you should be confident in are of course the basics, as soon as you feel comfortable in a fight employing these, you can begin broadening your repertoire with more complex techniques such as disarming (not dismemberment) or masterful defenses.
