# Time

How long will the duel take? Where real duels are over after one hit, in tournaments or sparring, it is a lot different. Will the duel take three minutes, when you try to score as many points as possible, or will it have a point limit, or an exchange limit. This is important to understand, mostly for yourself. Sparring and dueling is very intense, so depending on your style, you will have to dose your energy accordingly. If you are dueling during a set time and you are ahead in points, you can take it easy. But should the opposite be true then you’ll probably have to use more energy to engage to get more opportunities to gain points (but be mindful of those Droids). When a duel is based on venues, then you can relax more about time. Here, technique will reign supreme to win. But always keep in mind that the factor of time can also be applied to the number of fights you still have ahead of you, so don’t burn all your fuel in the first duel.
