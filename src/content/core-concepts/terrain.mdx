# Terrain

Where does the duel take place? Inside, or outside? On firm ground or a field of grass? Is the arena circular or square? Where is the sun located (or suns)? Is it hot or cold? These are all factors you can use to your advantage. If the arena is a square in shape, you can drive your opponent into a corner. On a sunny day, you can position yourself with the sun on your back, so it hinders your opponent’s sight. If you are successful in using the terrain to gain advantage over your opponent, you can safely say that you have the high ground!
